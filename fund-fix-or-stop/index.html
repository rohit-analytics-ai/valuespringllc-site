What is in the workbook

  • Self-assessment
  • Review board charter
  • AI inventory entry and discovery checklist
  • Intake and tiering form
  • Gateway control checklist and model selection record
  • Agent approval record and promotion checklist
  • Five-test scorecard and threshold sheet with the status rules
  • Quality and value measurement plans, with value leakage
  • Monthly cost report
  • Recovery plan and incident record
  • Explore entry scorecard and gate decision record
  • Leadership page and Red decision memo
  • 90-day plan tracker
  • General-purpose tool value plan and vendor checklist
  • Three completed examples: a threshold sheet, a Red decision memo and a value confirmation

Yellow cells are inputs. The other cells calculate, so each sheet can be reused with an organization’s own figures. The completed examples use the book’s fictional bank, and every figure in them is illustrative.

Use

Readers may copy and adapt the templates for use within their own organizations. The templates may not be sold or republished.
